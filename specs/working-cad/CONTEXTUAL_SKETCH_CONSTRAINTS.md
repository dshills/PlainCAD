# Contextual sketch relations

Selecting sketch geometry reveals shape-matching relations. One or more lines support Horizontal and Vertical; exactly two lines support Parallel and Perpendicular; exactly two points support Coincident; one line with a circle/arc, or two circles/arcs, supports Tangent. Preview supports at most 32 selected items and 512 sketch entities. Other relations remain in the existing Sketch tools.

A relation is first staged in an immutable project revision. Preview solves driving constraints and validates all downstream geometry through the isolated modeling worker. A bare sketch preview is labeled as a sketch solve and never claims a solid. Native bodies require successful OpenCascade assertions. Apply requires the exact project revision, file session, component, canvas and selection, together with the worker-issued result belonging to this specific proposal. Cancel and stale results never add history; Apply adds one Undo step.

Existing entity, dimension and constraint IDs and bound expressions are retained. Applying to a legacy validation-mode sketch explicitly activates driving solving; the preview lists this change so the new relation remains parametric. Authored geometry and the existing driving baseline are checked before solving the new relation. Existing conflicts, duplicate intent, lost closed regions, failed native modeling and unsupported selections produce a diagnostic. Existing constraints are never silently removed. Free solved coordinates are persisted as canonical seeds so Undo restores the authored shape independently of worker continuity caches.

Underconstrained geometry remains usable. Its displayed local degrees of freedom are a solver rank estimate, not a guarantee of globally unique geometry. Conflicting or nonconverged geometry requires repair before adding a relation; diagnostics name source constraint/entity IDs. Tangency is solver-bounded: arc contact must fall within the supported arc sweep. The tangent preview determines a valid nearby solution and does not offer a separate internal/external tangent mode.

Tests cover shape availability, observed orientation/dot/cross/tangent geometry, unchanged IDs, conflicts, worker-issued proof rejection, session/selection changes, Cancel and Undo. The browser acceptance test verifies a skewed non-template sketch constraint actually changes native extruded volume, preserves BRep validity/solid count, rejects a selection changed after Preview without history, and restores volume through Undo/Redo. Human usability and assistive technology validation remain separate from these automated checks.
